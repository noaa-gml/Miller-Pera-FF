# Miller-Pera 2026b (assumed) — comparison with GridFED and OCO-2 FF (ODIAC × TIMES)

All three products on the same 1° × 1° monthly grid in mol C m⁻² s⁻¹; global
totals use the Miller-Pera cell areas (spherical Earth, R = 6371.009 km).

* **GridFED v2025.1** — Global Carbon Budget gridded fossil emissions (Jones et al., 2021), 0.1° summed to 1° by `download_gridfed.py`; gross emissions (`TOTAL − CEMENT_CARBONATION`, i.e. excluding the cement carbonation sink, which CDIAC / Miller-Pera do not count).
* **OCO-2 FF v2026.2** — ODIAC fossil-fuel fluxes convolved with TIMES temporal factors (Basu & Nassar; the OCO-2 MIP prior), monthly means of the hourly files (`orion/make_oco2ff_monthly.py`); published through Feb 2026 (Mar–Apr 2026 are A. Jacobson's extrapolation, flagged `extrapolated`).
